# Ovelo landing page: research notes

Sources (Nov 2026): Granola, Linear, Raycast, Obsidian, Superhuman, Otter, Quizlet, and a dev.to 2026 conversion write-up. Patterns only; nothing copied.

## Rules applied

1. **Outcome first.** The headline says what the student gets (listen in class), not what the software is.
2. **One page, one offer, one button.** "Get Ovelo" repeats in the nav, hero and closing section. Everything else is a plain text link.
3. **Show the product above the fold.** A real-looking app mock built in HTML, not an illustration.
4. **Headline, subhead, button and visual all say the same thing.** The mock shows exactly what the headline promises.
5. **No fake proof.** An early product uses true facts (how it works, who built it) instead of invented logos, numbers or quotes.
6. **Answer objections where they come up.** Privacy gets its own section with a plain data-flow diagram; cost, devices and offline go in a short FAQ.
7. **Little text per section.** One heading, one or two sentences, one visual. The page is long enough to answer the questions and no longer.
8. **Fast by construction.** No frameworks, no images beyond the icon, no third-party scripts; fonts are the only outside request.
9. **Phone first for students.** Single column at 360px, large tap targets, the mock reflows instead of shrinking.
10. **Calm, specific voice.** Short declarative sentences ending in periods. No hype words.

## What was borrowed, and from where

- **Granola:** the before / during / after walkthrough became Record, Read, Ask; privacy stated in the hero and again in its own block.
- **Linear:** dark UI mock as the hero visual, mono figure-style labels, sentence-case declarative headings.
- **Raycast:** short headline that ends in a period; the same primary action repeated down the page with one label.
- **Obsidian:** local-first explained as three parallel "Your ..." claims with one supporting sentence each.
- **Superhuman:** one sentence per feature card; text density kept low.
- **Otter (what to avoid):** two competing hero buttons, repeated testimonials and logo walls, a long cluttered scroll. Ovelo has one button and no repetition.
- **Quizlet (what to avoid):** study modes buried in a sentence. Ovelo gives each tool its own named card with one line.
- **dev.to article:** specific button copy, one offer per page, real facts over borrowed credibility, keep mobile load light.
