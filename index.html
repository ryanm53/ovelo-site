00:03Privacy

Your audio stays on your computer.

Here is exactly what goes where.

Stays on your computer

  1. 01

    Your mic

    The lecture, as you hear it.

  2. 02

    Audio file

    Saved to your disk while you record. Never uploaded.

  3. 03

    Transcript

    Made on your device. No audio is sent out to do it.

Text only

Leaves your computer

  1. 04

    Text to the AI

    Words from your notes go to Claude, the AI model Ovelo uses, to write a summary, answer a question, or make a study tool.

  • Your recordings.

    Audio is a file on your own machine. Ovelo never sends it anywhere.

  • Your device does the listening.

    Speech is turned into text locally, not on someone else’s server.

  • Text, and only what is needed.

    The AI sees words, never sound. If you add a scanned page or a photo, that page is sent so it can be read.

Recording rules differ by school and by instructor. Follow yours, and ask before you record if you are not sure.

00:04Questions

Fair questions.

Is it private?

Your audio is saved on your own computer and is never uploaded. Transcription happens on your device. What leaves is text, sent to Claude so it can write a summary, answer a question, or make a study tool. A scanned page or photo you add yourself is also sent so it can be read.

Does recording work offline?

Yes. After first-time setup, recording and transcription run on your computer with no connection. You need the internet for summaries, answers, and the study tools.

What does it cost?

Pricing has not been announced yet. When it is, it will be on this page.

Which devices does it run on?

Today Ovelo runs on your own computer, which is where the recording and transcription happen. The exact list of supported devices is not final yet, so we are not going to guess here.

Is this a way to skip class?

No. It is built for the opposite. You go, you listen, and you stop typing every word. Later you study from what was said in your own lectures.

Am I allowed to record my lectures?

That depends on your school and your instructor. Check the rules and ask first if you are unsure. Ovelo keeps recordings on your computer, but permission is still yours to get.
